`metaGOflow` is a [Common Workflow Language (CWL)](https://commonwl.org/) based pipeline for the analysis of shotgun metagenomic data at the sample level. 
It was initially built to address the needs of the [EMO BON community](https://www.embrc.eu/services/emo-bon) but it can be used for any type of shotgun sequencing data. 

`metaGOflow` is based on the tools and subworkflow implemented in the framework of [MGnify](https://github.com/EBI-Metagenomics/pipeline-v5).
